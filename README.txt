Monitor and control your Vestel EVC04 or Webasto Unite charger locally over Modbus TCP. The Webasto Unite is built on the Vestel EVC04 and uses the same Modbus interface. Charge modes Fast, Minimum + solar and Solar only, automatic switching between 1 and 3 phases, and load balancing based on your P1 meter.

This is a community app and is not made by Vestel or Webasto.
